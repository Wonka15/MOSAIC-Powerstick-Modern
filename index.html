THE STORY BEHIND THE DEVICE

Origins, Kickstarter & engineering history

MOSAIC was more than a storage accessory. This archive preserves the crowdfunding story, the company behind it, and the people whose work helped bring it to life.

01

Kickstarter beginnings

In July 2015, MOSAIC was reported as a new PowerStick Kickstarter project: a portable battery, wireless media drive, and file-sharing hub. Contemporary coverage described a 4,500 mAh battery and support for streaming to multiple devices.

The company behind MOSAIC

The product was designed by PowerStick. The company's current public website continues to identify PowerStick and lists its team and Canadian headquarters. This is a record of the company connection, not a claim that every historical product or service remains supported.
