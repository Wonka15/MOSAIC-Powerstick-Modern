# MOSAIC research archive

This folder is the organized home for historical, product, and technical research. The website's home screen now provides a short link to the archive; the detailed story lives on the dedicated [Research Archive page](../../research.html).

## Archive structure

1. **Kickstarter and product timeline** — [kickstarter-and-timeline.md](kickstarter-and-timeline.md): campaign identity, contemporary launch coverage, reported specifications, timeline, conflicting reports, and unresolved campaign facts.
2. **Company and engineering credits** — [history-and-credits.md](../history-and-credits.md): PowerStick context, John Zhu attribution standards, supplied video sources, and preservation policy.
3. **Physical device and protocol** — [protocol-notes.md](../protocol-notes.md): PSTR2 label facts, observed device behavior, network/API facts, and unknowns.
4. **Original iOS app analysis** — [ios-app-analysis.md](ios-app-analysis.md): screen-by-screen walkthrough, safe app-data inspection, network observations, and behavior map.
5. **Wi-Fi launch and captive-portal feasibility** — [wifi-launch-and-captive-portal.md](wifi-launch-and-captive-portal.md): the intended website entry flow, browser limitations, and investigation plan.

## Evidence labels

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Primary source** | Original campaign, company material, device label, FCC filing, or original video/interview. |
| **Contemporary secondary source** | Reporting or review published around the product's launch. |
| **Owner observation** | A fact observed directly on a physical unit or supplied by its owner. |
| **Unverified** | A lead or claim that still needs a source. |

## Research rules

1. Record the source and date beside every historical claim.
2. Distinguish what a campaign promised from what shipped.
3. Do not treat a planned delivery date as proof of delivery.
4. Do not infer a person's MOSAIC-specific work from their company title.
5. Preserve conflicting specifications with their source context; do not silently choose one.
6. Never publish Wi-Fi passwords, private credentials, or private media.
