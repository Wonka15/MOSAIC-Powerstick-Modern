# MOSAIC history, Kickstarter & engineering credits

This document preserves the product's origin story and the people and organization associated with it. It is intentionally conservative: a company job title is not proof of a person's specific contribution to every product.

## The product

MOSAIC was presented in 2015 as a combination portable charger and wireless media-storage device. Contemporary launch coverage described a 4,500 mAh battery, flash-storage configurations, built-in Wi-Fi, and media sharing with multiple devices. The PSTR2 device label in this repository independently identifies the product as **MOSAIC POWERSTREAM**, branded **Powerstick.com**.

## Kickstarter contribution

Review its text and comments for milestones, delivery information, and named contributors before summarizing.

## Company

The 2015 coverage identifies PowerStick as the designer of MOSAIC. PowerStick's current official website is [powerstick.com](https://powerstick.com). Its current public positioning emphasizes branded merchandise, chargers, speakers, trackers, notebooks, and packaging; the site also says “PowerStick is evolving” and promotes its PWR+ mark. This documents the company's present-day public identity, which is broader than the original MOSAIC consumer gadget. A 2016 Ottawa Business Journal profile describes the company as founded by Nigel Harris and reports that MOSAIC was assembled in Ottawa at that time.

Sources:
- [PowerStick — current official website](https://powerstick.com)
- [PowerStick — Our Team](https://powerstick.com/our-team)
- [Ottawa Business Journal, “Feature: Powerstick, made in Ottawa, no kidding?” (November 26, 2016)](https://obj.ca/feature-powerstick-made-in-ottawa-no-kidding/)

**Important distinction:** the existence of a current PowerStick website and team page does not establish that the original MOSAIC app, cloud services, firmware, or product support are still maintained. Those remain separate research questions.

## Engineering credit: John Zhu

PowerStick's current team page identifies **John Zhu** as **Director of Engineering**. Public FCC equipment-authorization documents for PowerStick products also identify John Zhu as an engineering contact / responsible party. This is strong evidence of his engineering leadership role at PowerStick.

What is not yet established from the sources collected here is the exact scope of Zhu's work on the original MOSAIC / PSTR2 hardware, firmware, wireless stack, or companion app. The archive therefore credits his documented company role while leaving product-specific contributions open for confirmation from the supplied videos, campaign materials, interviews, or direct company records.

Sources:
- [PowerStick — Our Team](https://powerstick.com/our-team)
- [FCC equipment filing: PowerStick.com Inc. (2024)](https://fcc.report/FCC-ID/2AITN-PRESTO/7586842.pdf)
